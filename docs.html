Build a cosmic foundry, relight the expanse, and take a break with three optional side games.

Start with the tutorial below, or jump to the campaign, backups, and download status.

Your first five minutes

  1. Close the welcome card, then tap, click, or hold IGNITE to earn energy. With keyboard focus on the core, hold Space or Enter.
  2. At 12 J, open Build and buy a Spark collector. It earns 1 J each second without further tapping.
  3. Save 35 J for Conductive gloves in Research. Your first chapter requires both a collector and these gloves.
  4. Reinvest in machines and research. Follow Your next horizon and the six-chapter list, rather than waiting for the energy balance alone to advance the story.
  5. Explore the optional side games below the core. You can finish the campaign without any of them, without contracts, and without going supernova.

Use the Core, Build, Research, and Stars navigation shortcuts to move between stations. On a phone, the panels stack vertically. Settings (the gear) holds audio, motion, and save controls.

Controls & navigation

Use touch or mouse for buttons. Press Tab to move focus and Enter or Space to activate the focused control. Hold the core to generate repeated pulses; release to stop. Side-game controls use ordinary buttons, not dragging.

Expand an off-duty activity by selecting its title. Confirmation dialogs protect supernova, contract starts, imports, and reset; choose Keep playing to cancel. Press Escape to dismiss a dialog. Guide navigation links jump to sections; search filters sections, and Clear search restores them.

Side game: Reactor resonance

Select Tune reactor, watch the indicator, and activate the control again inside the mint target zone. Success grants 15 seconds of double automatic output. Every completed attempt starts a 30-second cooldown; a miss loses no energy.

Reduced motion slows the challenge, but it still requires visual timing. It is entirely optional.

Side games: Echo relay & Lightway

Echo relay

  1. Expand Echo relay and select Receive sequence.
  2. Study the four numbered signals for as long as you want.
  3. Select Hide sequence · ready to repeat, then press the numbered buttons in the same order.

A correct sequence wins; a wrong signal ends the attempt without removing energy. Either result starts a 90-second cooldown.

Lightway

Connect IN at the left edge of the top-left tile to OUT at the right edge of the bottom-right tile. Activate each tile to cycle through horizontal, vertical, and four corner shapes. Adjacent openings must connect; simply reaching the last tile is not enough unless it exits right.

Select Send a test pulse to check. Failed tests are free and you may keep adjusting. A solved route awards energy and starts a 90-second cooldown, then a new board awaits.

Each puzzle win grants 30 seconds of base automatic output, or at least 20 J. The temporary reactor boost does not multiply this reward. Puzzles have no countdown timer and do not risk your stored energy. Their cooldowns are separate and survive supernova. Unfinished puzzle rounds are not saved.

Supernova & permanent stars

At 25,000 run earnings you may go supernova. The award is floor(sqrt(run earnings / 25,000)): 25,000 earns 1 star, 100,000 earns 2, and 225,000 earns 3.

Each lifetime star adds 25% to base pulse and automatic power. Four stars give a 2× multiplier. Supernova resets energy, machines, research, run earnings, the active contract, and temporary output boosts. It preserves lifetime earnings, stars, constellation purchases, completed contracts, achievements, side-game wins, ending completion, cooldowns, and settings.

Spend stars without losing their power bonus

  • First light — 1 star: begin future runs with three collectors. It does not add collectors to the current run.
  • Resourceful hands — 2 stars: machines cost 20% less, including this run.
  • Living core — 3 stars: pulses gain 20% of one second of current automatic output.
  • Deep orbit — 5 stars: gardens, mills, and galaxy hearts produce twice as much.

Each ability is purchased once. Available essence decreases when spent; the lifetime-star power multiplier does not.

Contracts & achievements

Contracts unlock after your first star. Starting a contract resets current energy, machines, research, and run earnings without awarding stars. Permanent progress remains. Review the confirmation first. Leaving a contract removes its restriction and keeps the new run.

  • Silent skies: no manual pulse energy; start with a collector (three with First light). Earn 500,000 J this run for a permanent 20% automatic-output bonus.
  • Small constellation: own at most five of each machine; earn 2,000,000 J this run for a permanent 20% pulse-power bonus.

Objectives finish automatically once reached, remove the restriction, and award their bonus once. They are optional alternate runs, not campaign requirements.

Six logbook achievements

  • A helping hand: build a machine.
  • A million little lights: earn 1,000,000 J across all runs.
  • From the ashes: earn star essence.
  • Bright ideas: buy all seven research upgrades in one run.
  • Off-duty engineer: win each of the three side games.
  • No one left in the dark: launch the beacon.

Achievements record milestones; they do not award additional output bonuses.

Saving, backups & offline progress

Progress autosaves every 5 seconds in local storage. The hosted site, a local browser copy, and the desktop edition have separate storage. There is no account or cloud sync.

In Settings, choose Export save before changing devices, clearing storage, or installing another edition. Transfer the JSON file yourself and use Import save in the destination. Review the replacement confirmation. Valid version-1 starter saves migrate to the expanded game; malformed or unsupported saves are rejected.

Returning awards at most four hours of owned-machine production. Temporary boosts expire with elapsed time; offline progress does not buy equipment. Importing a save is not an anti-cheat system.

Erase local progress removes all progress, including permanent stars, after confirmation and awards nothing. This is different from supernova. Private browsing, storage restrictions, or clearing site data may erase saves; keep exported backups.

Web play & Windows downloads

Play in a browser on desktop or phone. No installation is required. The expanded campaign is published. Narrow-layout and browser interaction checks have been performed; physical-phone and native Windows verification are separate.

Windows release status: the unsigned installer, portable guide, Build Studio, and update package have been published. Source and artifact hashes were verified; installation, launch, and update-cycle behavior on a physical Windows computer remain unverified. Check official releases for verified published files and exact version notes. Do not assume source availability means a release has passed Windows testing.

Installing a published Windows release

  1. Download SparkFoundrySetup.exe from the official release and check its release notes and published checksum.
  2. The application is unsigned. Windows may show an unknown-publisher warning. Verify the origin; do not disable security protection to run an untrusted download.
  3. Run the installer and open Spark Foundry. The game uses local bundled assets and does not need a browser server.
  4. Export/import a save to move existing browser progress into the desktop edition.

Portable companion filenames are SparkFoundryGuide-VERSION.exe and SparkFoundryBuilder-VERSION.exe. The guide opens this handbook. Build Studio is for building trusted source, not a prerequisite for playing.

Desktop updates

The installed Windows game is designed to check the official unsigned Squirrel update feed at startup and periodically. Update status offers retry, Later, and Restart to install. A save handshake is required before restart. The Application menu provides manual checks and release notes. Portable companions and development runs do not install updates. End-to-end update delivery is not yet Windows-verified.

Settings & accessibility

Sound is off by default, and visual feedback does not depend on audio. Reduced motion respects your system preference and can be enabled in Settings. The interface supports keyboard focus, touch, responsive layouts, and browser zoom.

The two untimed puzzles can be played at your own pace. Reactor resonance still uses a moving indicator and may be skipped. These features are not a claim of a complete assistive-technology audit or physical-device certification.

Troubleshooting

A chapter will not advance

Check its machine and research requirements as well as run earnings. A locked purchase may require more lifetime-in-this-run earnings; an affordable-looking item may still be locked.

My save is missing

Use the original browser profile and exact site address. Desktop and browser storage are separate. Import an exported backup if available.

A file-opened page is blank

Browser modules need HTTP hosting. Open the hosted game or serve the repository with python -m http.server 8080 and visit http://localhost:8080. The packaged desktop app handles its own local asset loading.

The desktop update check fails

Keep playing, export a backup, and retry later. An unpublished feed or network failure is not evidence that you have the newest release. Do not replace files manually inside an installed application.

I cannot afford anything

Pulse the core or wait for machines. During Silent skies, pulses intentionally produce no energy. Leave the contract if you want unrestricted play.
