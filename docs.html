Getting started

  1. Generate your first energy. Activate the central reactor. Each activation earns energy immediately.
  2. Buy your first producer for 12 energy. It generates energy automatically, even when you are not tapping.
  3. Reinvest. Add producers, then buy upgrades as your foundry grows. Costs come out of your current energy balance.
  4. Try stabilization. The optional timing challenge briefly doubles automatic production.
  5. Reach 25,000 energy earned in a run. Prestige becomes available: restart with permanent star power.

There is no purchase system, advertisement, account, or server connection required for play. Play actively, leave the foundry running, or return later.

Controls

Use a mouse or touch to activate the reactor and purchase equipment. Interactive controls are keyboard-accessible: press Tab to move focus and Enter or Space to activate a focused button.

The stabilization button uses the same controls. Keyboard users do not need to point at a moving target; activate the button when the indicator reaches the marked zone.

Production & costs

Energy is your spendable balance. Your production rate is energy generated per second. Energy earned in the current run includes energy you have already spent; spending does not move the prestige goal backward.

Each additional producer of the same type costs 1.16 times the previous base price, rounded up. Different tiers have separate cost curves. The displayed rate includes your current upgrades, permanent stars, and active stabilization bonus.

A reactor activation starts at 1 energy. Upgrades and stars improve its yield. Automatic producers keep operating while the game is open; capped offline progress is calculated when you return.

Research & upgrades

Upgrade cards explain their effect, cost, and unlock requirement. Some research only becomes available after you have earned enough energy in the current run. Unlocking research does not purchase it: you still need enough spendable energy.

  • Conductive gloves: 35 energy; doubles base tap power.
  • Superconducting coils: 200 energy; doubles automatic production; unlocks at 100 run energy earned.
  • Resonant touch: 700 energy; adds 10% of one second of automatic production to each tap; unlocks at 500 run energy earned.
  • Plasma compression: 3,000 energy; doubles automatic production again; unlocks at 2,000 run energy earned.
  • Quantum fingertips: 8,000 energy; multiplies base tap power by five; unlocks at 5,000 run energy earned.

Upgrades are purchased once per run. They improve active generation or automatic production. Prestige clears purchased upgrades so you can build a more powerful foundry from the beginning.

Reactor stabilization

Stabilization is an optional timing challenge. Start the challenge, watch the moving indicator, and activate the control when it enters the highlighted success zone.

A successful stabilization grants double automatic production for 15 seconds. Every attempt, successful or not, starts a 30-second cooldown. A miss does not remove energy, equipment, or permanent progress. You never need to play this challenge to advance.

The production bonus is temporary. Stabilization does not permanently upgrade producers or increase prestige stars.

Prestige: start again, stronger

Once your run has earned at least 25,000 energy, you can restart it in exchange for stars. The amount awarded is:

floor(sqrt(run energy earned / 25,000))

That means 25,000 earned energy awards 1 star, 100,000 awards 2, and 225,000 awards 3. Each star permanently adds 25% to base active and automatic output. Four stars provide a 2× multiplier; eight provide 3×.

Prestige resets your energy, producers, purchased upgrades, run earnings, and temporary reactor bonuses. Your existing stars remain, new stars are added, and your settings are preserved. Review the confirmation before restarting.

Saving & offline progress

Your game saves automatically in this browser every 5 seconds. Saves are local to the current browser profile and site address, not synced to an account.

When you return, owned producers earn energy for up to 4 hours away. Temporary bonuses expire according to elapsed time; a 15-second boost does not last for hours offline. Offline progress does not buy producers or upgrades for you.

Export and import

Use the save controls to export a backup before clearing browser data, changing devices, or resetting your game. Import only a Spark Foundry save you trust. Imported saves are validated before replacing your current progress; keep a backup of your current game first.

Reset

The reset option erases local game progress, including permanent stars. It requires explicit confirmation. Reset is different from prestige: it awards no stars. A previously exported backup can restore that saved state.

Private browsing, blocked browser storage, or clearing site data can remove local progress. Export is the reliable way to keep a separate copy.

Settings & accessibility

Sound is off by default. Enable it only if you want audio feedback. All important information is also visible; audio is never required.

Reduced motion reduces decorative movement. Your system preference is respected, and a game setting is available. The optional timing challenge remains a visual timing activity; skip it if it is uncomfortable.

The layout adapts to narrow screens, controls support touch and keyboard, and focus indicators show your current keyboard position. You can zoom the browser to enlarge text.

Troubleshooting

My save disappeared

Check that you are using the same site address and browser profile. Local preview, a published site, and another browser each have separate storage. Import an exported backup if you have one.

An import was rejected

Use an unmodified export from this version of Spark Foundry. Invalid JSON, unsupported save versions, negative values, and malformed data are rejected to protect game state.

The page does not run when opened from a file

The game uses JavaScript modules. Serve the files through a local HTTP server or open the hosted game rather than double-clicking the HTML file.

I cannot afford anything

Generate energy manually or let existing producers accumulate it. A disabled purchase control means its cost or unlock requirement has not yet been met. There is no energy penalty for waiting.
